OVERVIEW

Halloween Fest was a poster created in promotion for an event that occurred on campus of La Salle University.
What makes this poster stand out was the social media campaign that surrounded it. While the poster itself is static, there was an animated video and Instagram Story template created alongside it to carry the message through the lens of social media.

Created Using: Adobe Photoshop, Adobe Illustrator, Blender, and Adobe After Effects.


About The Design

Inspired by dark grunge-art, Halloween Fest served as an exploration of 3D typography, and illustration.

The typography was initially created in Adobe Illustrator, but was brought into Blender for a unique 3D look. The skull imagery was also created in Blender, and later edited in Adobe Photoshop.

The final design was created in Adobe Photoshop.
