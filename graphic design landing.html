THE BACKSTAGE PASS MAGAZINE

From Digital Design to Print

The Backstage Pass Magazine was developed as my senior capstone project (Class of 2026), serving as a time-capsule of my four years as a host and producer of Backstage Pass, a student-run television show through La Salle TV.

The project functions as both a personal reflection and a brand exploration, translating a televised entertainment show into a tangible cohesive print experience.

Halloween Fest

Poster Design

Halloween Fest is a poster created in promotion for an event that occurred on the campus of La Salle University.

What makes this poster stand out was the social media campaign that surrounded it. While the poster itself is static, there was an animated video and Instagram Story template created alongside it to carry the message through the lens of social media.

BRIGHTER DAYS AHEAD

Personal Poster Design

Brighter Days Ahead is one of many personal projects created in personal exploration of texture, typography, and layout.

These personal projects are typically posters regarding popular music artists, which are typically created to be shared on social media.

CAMPUS ENGAGEMENT CENTER

Re-Brand

During the summer of 2025, I was asked by La Salle University's Campus Engagement Center leader to lead a re-brand for the program.

The program leader requested a design that felt corporate and serious, but also fun and playful. It was also noted that the logo be as simple and recognizable as possible.

LATE NIGHT LA SALLE

Re-Brand

While undergoing a rebrand for The Campus Engagement Center during the summer of 2025, I was simultaneously working on a re-brand for another program that works alongside The Campus Engagement Center; Late Night La Salle.

The program's leader requested that the logo provide a fun and "cool" vibe, while still being similar to previous Late Night La Salle logos.

SIBERIAN HUSKY

Infographic Design

This Siberian Husky infographic explores the characteristics, fun-facts, and temperament of the Siberian Husky, translating detailed information into a visually engaging and quickly digestible format.

The goal was to take a data-heavy subject and translate it into an infographic that can easily be understood.
