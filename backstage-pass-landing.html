BACKSTAGE PASS - THE MAGAZINE

The Backstage Pass Magazine was developed as my senior capstone project (Class of 2026), serving as a time-capsule of my four years as a host and producer of Backstage Pass, a student-run television show through La Salle TV.
The project functions as both a personal reflection and a brand exploration, translating a televised entertainment show into a tangible cohesive print experience.



This project served as a time-capsule of the four years spent working on the La Salle TV show, but also as a showcase of the skills I've learned and honed during my four years attending La Salle University.
This project showcases my skill in graphic design, typography, photography, video, and copywriting.
The goal of this project was to explore balance between structure and creative freedom, but also the constraints of editorial design.
A key focus was building a consistent system that would portray the show through a brand across 124 pages total while allowing moments to break that structure for both emphasis and visual interest.
